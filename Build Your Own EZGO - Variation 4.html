      Frequently Asked Questions

      Everything you need to know before you build.

      Is this an online purchase?
      No. The builder is a planning tool to help you compare options and understand pricing. When you are happy with your build, send it with Get a Quote and a Guild specialist will confirm availability and final pricing with you.
      What does “standard and moderately used” mean?
      Every 2019–2022 EZGO RXV Elite base model ships with the used body, seats and tires it came with. Quality and wear vary by current inventory, so call us at 503-208-3394 for options and photos, or upgrade any of them to brand new.
      Can I pick a colour if I keep the used body?
      Used body colours depend on what is in stock that week. If colour matters, a brand new body (+$1,000) comes in Platinum, Inferno Red, Patriot Blue, Electric Blue, White, Charcoal or Black.
      Which accessories do I need to carry four people?
      Add the rear seat kit for two extra rear-facing passengers. Most owners pair it with the extended roof (built for four passengers) and seat belts. The builder will suggest these when you add a rear seat.
      How is delivery priced?
      Picking up from one of our shops is free. Delivery is $150 per hour of drive time from the nearest Guild shop (Portland HQ, Las Vegas, Bend or Seattle) to you. The builder shows an estimate; we confirm the exact time with your quote.

      Need more help?
      Our friendly team is available to answer any other questions or provide additional details. Feel free to reach out for personalized assistance.
